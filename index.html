Questions

Good to know

Do I need to be home?

No. Many customers leave a key or a door code. Add entry instructions at checkout and we'll take it from there.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes. We bring vacuums, mops, microfiber cloths and pet-safe products. If you prefer we use your products, add a note at checkout.

How does the recurring discount work?

Weekly, every-2-weeks and monthly plans get 20%, 15% and 10% off the Standard clean on every visit. Cancel or pause anytime.

When am I charged?

After your clean is finished. You'll get a receipt by email with the checklist your team completed.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free changes up to 24 hours before your visit. Later cancellations have a $40 fee to cover your team's time.
